Work

Work

A few areas I have worked on over the past several years.

PET nanoplastics and aggregation

Modelling PET nanoplastics, including contaminant binding, colloidal behaviour, zeta potential, and aggregation.

This has involved molecular simulation, Python-based analysis, and comparison with simpler colloidal models such as DLVO where those approximations still hold.

Open full app

Gold nanoparticle sensor design

Computational design and prioritisation of functional monolayers on gold nanoparticles for selective metabolite recognition.

The work combined molecular modelling, exploratory data analysis, and regularised models for small experimental datasets, with a focus on interpretable design rules.

Peptides on gold nanoparticles

PhD work on folded and constrained peptides in dense monolayers on gold nanoparticles.

The work combined experiment and computation, including peptide synthesis and characterisation, spectroscopy, chromatography, molecular dynamics, and enhanced sampling.

Exploratory technical work

On the side, I occasionally work on smaller technical problems outside my main research area, including time-series prediction, ARC-style reasoning tasks, and simple agent or population-dynamics models.
