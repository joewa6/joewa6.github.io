\section{Professional Summary}

\cvitem{}{Computational chemist focused on structure-guided molecular
modelling, ligand prioritisation, and physically grounded analysis across
biomolecular systems. Currently building reproducible ligand-screening,
docking, rescoring, and scientific Python workflows that support the cycle from
screening to binding-mode inspection, rationalisation, and prioritisation.
Strong focus on benchmark-aware evaluation and interpretable computational
outputs for discovery-relevant decisions.}
